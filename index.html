OpenAir

A personal health dashboard for Android. It turns the data from your fitness band into recovery, strain, sleep, Body Age and more, and keeps every byte on your phone.

  • Reads your health data from Android Health Connect, on the phone.
  • Optionally, and only if you turn it on, reads four readings that Google Health doesn’t share with Health Connect: blood oxygen (SpO2), VO2 max, Active Zone Minutes and irregular rhythm notifications. Read-only.
  • No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. Nothing is sold or shared.

OpenAir is a personal project, used by its owner. It is not a medical device.
